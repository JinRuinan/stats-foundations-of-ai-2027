About the workshop

Statistics for AI that can be trusted.

AI is rapidly reshaping scientific discovery, industry, and public life — yet the speed of progress can obscure the statistical principles that enable these methods, and the mechanisms behind their failures.

Today's AI systems can be difficult to validate, brittle in deployment, and poorly calibrated about what they do and do not know, raising pressing questions for high-stakes use. This workshop highlights principled ways that statistical thinking — modeling, inference, uncertainty quantification, and theory — can deepen our understanding of modern AI.

The emphasis is on frameworks that deliver conceptual clarity, practical diagnostics, and, where possible, meaningful guarantees that place AI's capabilities on firmer scientific footing. The program mixes invited talks with extended discussion and informal collaboration among a small group of researchers.

Topics

Where statistics meets the frontier of AI.

Six threads run through the program. Hover a card for a longer description; click it to open a selected reading.

  1. 01

    Trustworthy AI

    Reliability, robustness, invariance, interpretability, calibration, and the evaluation of AI systems in high-stakes settings.

    Selected reading ↗
  2. 02

    Uncertainty quantification

    Predictive uncertainty, Bayesian and frequentist perspectives, and decision-making under uncertainty.

    Selected reading ↗
  3. 03

    Transformers & architectures

    The mathematics of modern deep learning architectures, including transformers and transformer-next models.

    Selected reading ↗
  4. 04

    Pretraining & alignment

    The mathematics of pretraining, post-training, and alignment of large models.

    Selected reading ↗
  5. 05

    Diffusion models

    Theoretical foundations of diffusion and discrete diffusion models, and what they reveal about generative learning.

    Selected reading ↗
  6. 06

    Reasoning & emergence

    Formal characterizations of chain-of-thought reasoning and other emergent behaviors in modern AI.

FAQ

Practical questions.

Who can attend the workshop?

Attendance is by invitation. The workshop is designed for invited speakers, poster presenters, and a limited number of participants from MBZUAI and the wider research community. If you would like to attend, email the organizing team.

Is there a registration fee?

There is no registration fee. Attendance is by invitation, and capacity is limited.

Is the workshop in person?

Yes. The workshop will be held in person at MBZUAI's Masdar City campus in Abu Dhabi.

Can I present a poster?

A poster session is planned. Poster invitations are handled by the organizing team; details will be announced here. You can also register your interest by email.

Will talks be recorded?

Select sessions may be recorded and published after the workshop, subject to each speaker's consent. Recording details will be confirmed closer to the event.

Does the workshop cover travel and accommodation?

For invited speakers, the organizers arrange travel and accommodation. Other participants are responsible for their own travel arrangements.

Who should I contact?

Email ai.nexus@mbzuai.ac.ae with the subject line “Stats Foundations of AI 2027”, or contact the chair, Qiang Sun, through MBZUAI.
