What you will need

Your name and email, your affiliation, a homepage or Google Scholar profile, and optionally 2–3 representative works. You can also tell us whether you would like to present a poster and, if so, a working poster title.

Who should register

Researchers interested in attending, poster presenters, and guests from academia and industry. Invited speakers do not need to register again.

What happens next

The organizing team will review responses and follow up by email. Submitting the form expresses interest; it does not guarantee a place.
